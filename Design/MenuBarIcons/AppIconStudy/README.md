# App icon–inspired menu bar concepts

These monochrome SVG design masters reinterpret the existing BeszelBar app icon's bold, squared-off B with rounded right-hand bowls. They use `currentColor` and a 24 × 24 canvas.

1. **Original B** retains the solid silhouette and two counters of the app icon.
2. **Outline B** reduces the mark to a lighter, continuous line.
3. **Signal B** turns the two bowls into stacked, rounded bands, suggesting server activity while preserving the B.

The preview compares the app icon and the three concepts at approximate menu bar size, on both light and dark backgrounds. These are design candidates and are not connected to the app.
